## 1. Background

1.1 The Licensor has designed and developed a marketing website and a web
application for the Licensee's forensic science education business.

1.2 The Licensee has paid the Licensor **₹50,000 (Rupees Fifty Thousand only)**
for that development work.

1.3 The Parties did not previously record in writing what rights that payment
carried. This Agreement records those rights, and is intended to reflect what
both Parties understood the arrangement to be: that the Licensee receives a
fully working, operational platform for its business.

---

## 2. Definitions

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Software** | The marketing website and the web application described in Schedule A, in object/deployed form |
| **Source Code** | The human-readable programming code, database migrations, configuration and build scripts from which the Software is produced |
| **Deployment** | The live, running instance of the Software operated for the Licensee |
| **Licensee Data** | All data entered into or generated by the Deployment — student records, enrolments, course content, notes, payments, communications and analytics |
| **Support Period** | The period during which the Licensor provides the services in Clause 6 |

## 3. Grant of Licence

3.1 The Licensor grants to the Licensee a **perpetual, non-exclusive,
non-transferable, royalty-free licence** to use the Software for the operation
of the Licensee's education business.

3.2 This licence is **perpetual and does not depend on the Licensee paying any
support fee.** If support under Clause 6 lapses or is cancelled, the Licensee
may continue to use the Software indefinitely.

> This is deliberate. A licence that switches off when a fee is missed is a
> hostage arrangement, and it would put the Licensee's students at risk of
> losing access to material they have paid for.

3.3 The licence covers use by the Licensee's own staff and educators, and use by
the Licensee's students as end users.

---

## 4. Ownership of Intellectual Property

4.1 The Licensor **retains all right, title and interest** in the Software and
the Source Code, including copyright, and including all designs, architecture,
database schema and documentation created in producing it.

4.2 Nothing in this Agreement transfers ownership of the Source Code to the
Licensee. The Licensee's rights are limited to those in Clause 3.

4.3 The Licensor may reuse the underlying components, patterns and techniques in
other projects, **provided** no Licensee Data, Licensee branding, or content
written by the Licensee is reused.

---

## 5. Licensee Data — belongs to the Licensee

5.1 **All Licensee Data is and remains the exclusive property of the Licensee.**
The Licensor claims no ownership over it.

5.2 The Licensor will not use, sell, share or disclose Licensee Data other than
as necessary to operate and support the Deployment.

5.3 On written request at any time, and within **15 working days**, the Licensor
will provide the Licensee a complete export of all Licensee Data in a standard,
machine-readable format (SQL dump and/or CSV). This right survives termination
of this Agreement and is not conditional on any payment being up to date.

5.4 For the purposes of the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, the
Licensee is the **Data Fiduciary** in respect of its students' personal data and
is responsible for its own privacy notice and consents. The Licensor acts as a
**Data Processor** on the Licensee's documented instructions.

5.5 The Licensor shall:
- process Licensee Data only as needed to operate and support the Deployment;
- apply reasonable security safeguards to it;
- notify the Licensee **without undue delay, and in any event within 72 hours**,
  on becoming aware of any personal data breach;
- delete or return Licensee Data on the Licensee's written instruction, save
  where retention is required by law.

5.6 The Licensor shall not withhold Licensee Data, or access to it, on account
of any fee dispute or for any other commercial reason.

> 5.6 is deliberate and is in both Parties' interest. Student records are not a
> bargaining instrument, and treating them as one would expose the Licensor to
> liability well beyond the value of this Agreement.

## 6. Support and Maintenance

### 6.1 Included free period

The Licensor will provide the support services below **free of charge for twelve
(12) months** from the Effective Date.

### 6.2 After the free period

From month 13 onward, support continues at the Licensee's option at:

| Plan | Fee |
|---|---|
| Monthly | **₹1,000 per month** |
| Annual (paid in advance) | **₹12,000 per year** |

Fees are payable in advance. The Licensor may revise these rates with **60 days'
written notice**, not more than once in any 12-month period.

### 6.3 What support includes

- Fixing defects in the Software (things that do not work as built)
- Security patches and dependency updates
- Compatibility updates when Supabase, Vercel, Razorpay or AWS change their
  platforms in a way that breaks the Deployment
- Response to reported issues within **2 working days**
- Up to **2 hours per month** of minor changes (text, prices, images, settings)

### 6.4 What support does NOT include

The following are chargeable separately, quoted in advance and only started once
the Licensee approves in writing:

- New features or new pages
- Redesign or visual rework
- Data migration from other systems
- Training sessions beyond initial handover
- Work caused by the Licensee or a third party altering the Deployment,
  infrastructure or configuration
- More than 2 hours of minor changes in a month

**Rate for additional work: ₹1,200 per hour**, or a fixed quote for larger
pieces.

### 6.5 If support lapses

The Licensee may continue using the Software (Clause 3.2). The Licensor is under
no obligation to fix defects, apply security patches or respond to incidents.
Support may be resumed later at the then-current rate.

> The Licensee should understand the practical effect: an unmaintained web
> application accumulates security vulnerabilities, and platform changes will
> eventually break it. Lapsing support is a real risk, not a formality.

7.1 The Deployment depends on third-party services which are billed separately
and are **not covered by the support fee**:

| Service | Purpose | Approx. cost |
|---|---|---|
| Supabase | Database, authentication | Free tier, then from ~₹2,000/mo |
| Vercel | Hosting | Free tier, then ~₹1,700/mo |
| Amazon SES | Email delivery | ~₹80/mo at 200 students |
| Razorpay | Payments | Per-transaction fee |

7.2 As at the Effective Date, these accounts are held as follows:

| Service | Account holder |
|---|---|
| Amazon Web Services / SES | Licensee |
| Razorpay | Licensee |
| Domain registrar and DNS | Licensee |
| Supabase | **Licensor** (see 7.4) |
| Hosting (Vercel) | To be established under Clause 7.5 |

7.3 The Licensee is responsible for keeping the accounts in its name funded. The
Licensor is not liable for downtime caused by non-payment to a third-party
provider.

### 7.4 Transfer of the database account

7.4.1 The Supabase project currently sits in an account held by the Licensor.
Within **30 days** of this Agreement being signed, the Licensor shall transfer
that project to a Supabase organisation owned by the Licensee, and the Licensee
shall grant the Licensor administrative access to continue providing support.

7.4.2 The Parties record why: the project holds the Licensee's students'
personal data. The Licensee is the Data Fiduciary for that data and must not be
dependent on a single individual's login for access to its own business records.

7.4.3 Transfer of the account does **not** transfer any right in the Source Code
or in the database schema, functions or policies, which remain the Licensor's
property under Clause 4 and are subject to the restrictions in Clause 8.

### 7.5 Hosting

7.5.1 The Parties shall agree in writing, before the first production
deployment, whether the hosting account is held by the Licensor or the Licensee.

7.5.2 Where the Licensor holds it, the hosting cost shall be recharged to the
Licensee at cost plus 15%, or included in an agreed support fee.

## 8. Restrictions

The Licensee shall not, and shall not permit any third party to:

8.1 Copy, reproduce or distribute the Source Code;

8.2 Reverse engineer, decompile or disassemble the Software, except to the
extent this restriction is prohibited by applicable law;

8.3 Sell, rent, lease, sublicense or otherwise make the Software available to
any third party as a product or service, other than providing access to its own
students as end users;

8.4 Remove or obscure any copyright or ownership notice;

8.5 Use the Software to operate a competing platform for any other education
business;

8.6 Extract, download, copy or retain the Source Code from the hosting
infrastructure, build artefacts, deployment logs or any version control system,
notwithstanding that the Licensee holds the account under which the Deployment
runs.

> 8.6 exists because the infrastructure accounts are in the Licensee's name
> (Clause 7.2), which means the Licensee has technical access to deployment
> files. That access is granted so the Licensee is never locked out of its own
> business — it is not a grant of rights over the Source Code, and this clause
> says so explicitly rather than leaving it to be argued about later.

8.7 Engage any third party to perform work on the Software or the Source Code
without the Licensor's prior written consent, save where Clause 10 (Escrow) has
been triggered or a buyout under Clause 9 has completed.

## 9. Source Code Buyout (Optional)

9.1 The Licensee may, at any time, purchase full ownership of the Source Code
for **₹2,50,000 (Rupees Two Lakh Fifty Thousand only)**, less any support fees
already paid in the preceding 12 months.

9.2 On payment in full, the Licensor shall:
- assign all copyright in the Source Code to the Licensee in writing;
- deliver the complete repository, database migrations and deployment
  documentation;
- transfer or hand over administrative access to all infrastructure;
- provide up to **8 hours** of handover assistance.

9.3 After a buyout, this Agreement terminates except for Clauses 5, 11 and 12.

---

## 10. Source Code Escrow (Optional)

10.1 At the Licensee's written request and cost, the Licensor shall deposit a
current copy of the Source Code with a mutually agreed third-party escrow agent.

10.2 The escrow agent shall release the Source Code to the Licensee only if:
- the Licensor ceases business or becomes unable to provide support; or
- the Licensor fails to respond to a written support request for **30
  consecutive days** while support fees are paid and current.

10.3 On release, the Licensee receives a perpetual licence to use and modify the
Source Code for its own business only, and Clause 8.3 and 8.5 continue to apply.

> Clause 10 exists so the Licensee is not exposed to a single individual
> disappearing. It is the fair answer to "what if something happens to you",
> and costs the Licensor nothing to offer.

## 11. Warranties and Liability

11.1 The Licensor warrants that the Software was developed by the Licensor and
does not knowingly infringe any third party's intellectual property.

11.2 The Software is otherwise provided **"as is"**. The Licensor does not
warrant that it will be uninterrupted or entirely free of defects.

11.3 The Licensor's total aggregate liability under this Agreement shall not
exceed **the total fees paid by the Licensee in the 12 months preceding the
claim**.

11.4 Neither Party is liable for indirect or consequential loss, including loss
of profit or loss of business.

11.5 Nothing in this Agreement limits liability for fraud, wilful misconduct, or
any liability that cannot be limited under Indian law.

## 12. Confidentiality

12.1 Each Party shall keep confidential any non-public information of the other
Party obtained in connection with this Agreement, and shall not disclose it
without written consent.

12.2 This obligation survives termination for **three (3) years**.

## 13. Term and Termination

13.1 This Agreement begins on the Effective Date and continues until terminated.

13.2 Either Party may terminate the **support arrangement** on 30 days' written
notice. The **licence in Clause 3 survives** such termination.

13.3 The Licensor may suspend support (but not the licence) if support fees
remain unpaid **30 days** after a written reminder.

13.4 On termination for any reason, Clauses 4, 5, 8, 11 and 12 survive.
