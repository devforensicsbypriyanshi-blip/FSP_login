# What you get — in plain English

_A one-page summary of the Licence and Support Agreement. If anything here
disagrees with the signed agreement, the agreement wins._

---

## What you have paid for

₹50,000 covered building the marketing website and the full web application —
student portal, educator studio, admin console, payments, email, everything
listed in Schedule A.

## What you own

**Your platform, running, forever.** You can use it for as long as you like.
That does not stop if you cancel support, and it does not expire.

**All your data.** Every student record, every course, every payment, every note
you write — yours, completely. You can ask for a full export at any time and
receive it within 15 working days. That right never expires and is not tied to
any payment.

## What Veer owns

**The source code.** The programming behind the platform stays with Veer, the
same way you licence Tally or Zoom rather than owning them.

You have not lost anything you were using. You have the working platform; what
stays with Veer is the code that produces it.

## Support

**Free for the first 12 months.** Bug fixes, security updates, keeping it
working when Supabase, Razorpay or AWS change something, plus up to 2 hours a
month of small changes.

**After that, your choice:**

| | |
|---|---|
| Monthly | ₹1,000 |
| Yearly (paid upfront) | ₹12,000 |

**You can stop paying and keep using the platform.** You just would not get
fixes or security updates — and that is a genuine risk over time, not a
formality. Web applications do break when the platforms underneath them change.

New features are quoted separately at ₹1,200/hour, always agreed in writing
before any work starts.

## Running costs — separate, and in your name

Hosting, database, email and payments are billed by those companies directly to
you, in accounts registered in **your** name:

| | Roughly |
|---|---|
| Supabase (database) | free to start |
| Vercel (hosting) | free to start |
| Amazon SES (email) | ~₹80/month at 200 students |
| Razorpay | per transaction |
| Domain | ~₹1,000/year |

They are in your name deliberately, so you are never locked out of your own
infrastructure.

## Two things that protect you

**Escrow.** If you want it, a copy of the code can be lodged with a neutral
third party. If Veer ever becomes unreachable or stops responding for 30 days
while you are paid up, you get the code. You are not dependent on one person
staying available.

**Buyout.** If you ever want to own the code outright, you can buy it for
₹2,50,000, minus any support fees paid in the previous year. Then it is entirely
yours.

---

## In one line

You own your platform and your data and can use both forever. Veer keeps the
code, keeps it working for a year at no cost, and after that it is ₹1,000 a
month if you want him to carry on.
